# LinkVault Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

LinkVault ("the extension") helps you save and organise links. This policy explains what data it handles.

## What we collect
We do not collect, receive or store any of your data. LinkVault has no server, no analytics and no advertising.

## What is stored, and where
- Your links, collections, sections, tags and settings are stored in your browser using the browser's extension storage on your device.
- If you are signed in to your browser with sync enabled, the browser may sync this data to your own browser account (for example, your Google account) so it is available on your other devices. This is handled by the browser, not by us.
- Links marked "private" stay in your own storage and are never included in share links you create.

## Network requests the extension makes
- Site icons: to show a small icon for each saved website, the extension requests an icon image from Google's favicon service (google.com/s2/favicons) using only the website's domain name.
- Optional page access: if you click "Fill title from page" or "Check links", the extension asks for permission to access websites. When granted, it makes requests directly from your browser to the saved links to read a page title or check whether the page still loads. The results are stored only on your device.

- Link previews: if you click "Load preview" in the details panel, the extension fetches the page's preview image directly from that website to display it. It is not stored.

## Sharing
Share links are generated on your device and contain a copy of the shared collection's public links encoded in the link itself. Whoever you give the link to can read that copy. Because no server is involved, we cannot see or revoke these links.

## Permissions
The extension requests only the permissions needed for its features: storage, activeTab, contextMenus, clipboardRead and clipboardWrite, plus optional access to websites as described above.

## Children
LinkVault is not directed at children and collects no personal information.
